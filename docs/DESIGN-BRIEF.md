# Design brief

Preserve the incumbent soft editorial wellness language: ivory, limestone, espresso, clay, restrained orange, thin rules, generous whitespace, and sculptural campaign imagery. The design should feel Beirut-specific through warm limestone, linen, intimate proportions, and bilingual-ready information density—not like a generic SaaS landing page.

Desktop uses broad editorial compositions and a landscape hero. Mobile receives a portrait hero crop, darker bottom gradient, compact utility grid, and touch-first controls rather than a simple desktop stack.

All people and locations shown are fictional concept visualizations. No operating-studio, credential, testimonial, or outcome claim is made.

# FORME concept product

FORME is a fictional Spline concept demonstrating a complete boutique movement-studio website: classes, instructors, timetable filtering, calendar export, package comparison, booking and waitlist flows, policies, and direct contact with Spline.

Visitors should be able to understand the studio model and test the journeys without mistaking FORME for an operating business. Form submissions are validated and acknowledged but personal data is not retained. No booking, waitlist place, payment, or notification is created.
